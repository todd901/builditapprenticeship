Six tracks, one deal

Every home sale touches all six. Someone lists it, someone underwrites it, someone insures it, someone lends on it, someone builds or fixes it, and someone brings in the buyer online. Learn one track or stack several.

B

Brokerage

Real estate sales and management. This is where most people start, because it's the fastest license to earn and every other track feeds deals to it.

What you'll learn

  • Residential sales and listings
  • Commercial real estate
  • Rentals and property management
  • First-time homebuyer programs
  • Prospecting and client follow-up

Credentials

  • NYS real estate salesperson license
  • Associate broker and broker license
  • Continuing education

Where it leads

  • Licensed agent at a partner brokerage
  • Team leader or listing specialist
  • Property manager
  • Your own brokerage
U

Underwriting

The people who decide whether a deal closes. Learn to read a file the way lenders, appraisers and title companies do, and you'll be valuable on every side of the table.

What you'll learn

  • Mortgage underwriting and loan processing
  • Appraisal and property valuation
  • Title search and closing
  • Risk review for commercial files

Credentials

  • Loan processor training
  • NYS appraiser trainee license
  • Title and closing training

Where it leads

  • Loan processor or junior underwriter
  • Appraiser trainee, then licensed appraiser
  • Title closer
I

Insurance and Investments

Property and casualty, life, health, and the securities side. Every house you sell, every truck on a job site and every small business in our chapters needs coverage, which makes this the most renewable income in the program: you sell a policy once and it pays you every year it stays on the books. We have securities-licensed professionals on the direct team who mentor apprentices through the exams and handle sponsorship.

What you'll learn

  • Homeowners, auto and commercial coverage
  • Life and health insurance
  • Annuities and retirement products
  • Investment products and 529 plans

Credentials

  • NYS property & casualty license
  • NYS life, accident & health license
  • SIE exam
  • Series 6, Series 7 and Series 63

Where it leads

  • Licensed insurance agent
  • Registered representative
  • Agency producer, then your own agency
L

Lending

Mortgage, commercial, SBA and private money, plus credit repair. Nothing closes without financing, and the people who arrange it are paid on every deal.

What you'll learn

  • Residential mortgage origination
  • Commercial and SBA lending
  • Private and hard-money lending
  • Credit restoration and mortgage-ready coaching
  • Business credit building

Credentials

  • NMLS mortgage loan originator license
  • SBA lending program training
  • Commercial lending and broker certificates
  • Credit consultant certification and compliance training

Where it leads

  • Loan officer at a partner lender
  • Commercial and SBA loan broker
  • Credit restoration specialist or your own credit practice
  • Business capital advisor inside our chapters
  • Private lender placing your own or investor capital

Credit repair is the easiest door in this track: low startup cost, and the demand never runs out, because a buyer who gets turned down is a buyer who becomes a client. You'll be trained on the federal and state rules that govern this work, including what you may charge and when, before you take a single client.

Our founder has funded hundreds of real estate projects as a private lender. This track teaches the deal from the lender's chair, not just the borrower's.

D

Development

Land, flips, new construction and the construction trades. Contractors across the country can't find enough skilled workers, which means a trade learned now is a career that's waiting for you.

What you'll learn

  • Finding and evaluating land and properties
  • Fix-and-flip from purchase to resale
  • New construction and project management
  • Estimating, permits and scheduling
  • Hands-on trade skills

Credentials

  • Home improvement contractor license
  • OSHA 10 and OSHA 30
  • EPA lead-safe (RRP) certification
  • Trade apprenticeships and certifications

Where it leads

  • Skilled tradesperson
  • Project manager or site supervisor
  • Licensed contractor
  • Developer and investor

Our founder is both a licensed real estate broker and a licensed contractor, with more than 400 home rehabilitations since 1992. You'll learn from someone who has done the work on both sides.

Questions

What people ask before they apply.

Do I need experience?

No. The free start is built for people who are new to all six industries. Your mentor meets you where you are.

Is this a paid job?

This is a training and mentorship program, not an hourly job. Once you're licensed, you earn from the deals and clients you work, and the book of business you build is yours.

Can I do more than one track?

Yes. Many apprentices start in Brokerage or Insurance and add Lending or Development later. The tracks are designed to work together.

Does the program cover securities or investments?

Yes, on the Insurance and Investments track. Insurance is licensed by the state and securities is regulated by FINRA, so they're two separate paths. You can sit for the SIE exam on your own, and the registration exams after it require a sponsoring firm. We have securities-licensed professionals on the direct team who mentor apprentices through the exams and handle that sponsorship side. Most people license in insurance first and add securities once they have a book of clients.

Is credit repair included?

Yes, as part of the Lending track. You'll learn credit restoration, mortgage-ready coaching and business credit building, and you'll be trained on the compliance rules before working with clients.

How do scholarships work?

Apply through the form below and tell us about your goals. Scholarships can reduce or cover training and help with course and exam fees for applicants who qualify.

Where are classes held?

Our office is in Freeport, New York, with training on job sites, at closings and online.
